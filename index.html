Field Research Findings

From Paper Notebooks to Digital Clarity

Team Horizon interviewed business owners across Kijitonyama, Sinza, Makumbusho, and Mwenge to map out real daily bottlenecks.

The Manual Problem

What SME owners face every evening

  • Slow & Error-Prone Bookkeeping: Manual calculations in physical ledgers lead to miscalculated daily profits and unrecorded expenses.
  • High Risk of Data Loss: Water spills, fire, torn or lost notebooks cause years of financial history and debtor lists to disappear forever.
  • Unmonitored Debts & Unplanned Stockouts: Business owners struggle to track who still owes them (Wadeni) or when stock runs dangerously low.
  • Lack of Staff Accountability: Employees alter sales records or cost prices without an audit trail for the shop manager.

The Biashara Kiganjani Solution

Validate • Compute • Decide

  • Instant Transaction Validation: Autofills items and validates price input to eliminate mistyped amount errors during POS entry.
  • Automated Computation & Cloud Backups: Computes real-time profit, stock value, and debtor totals with 100% secure PostgreSQL cloud syncing.
  • ESMA AI Advisor (Gemini): Evaluates financial health scores and delivers actionable advice (restock warnings, debt reminders) in Swahili or English.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Managers retain control over analytics, reports, and settings while cashiers only see POS entry.
Comprehensive Capabilities

Designed for Daily Shop Operations

Every feature in Biashara Kiganjani was built following hands-on field research with retail shops, printing centers, mobile money agents, hardware stores, and pharmacies across Dar es Salaam.

POS & Multi-Mode Sales

Record daily Cash, Credit, or Mixed payment sales instantly. Prevent cashier entry errors with built-in price validation and automated item search.

Inventory & Restock Alerts

Real-time quantity tracking, damaged goods logging, supplier purchase entry, automated low-stock warnings, and identification of poor-moving goods.

Debt & Debtor Tracker

Track customers who bought on credit (Wadeni) with expected payment return dates, phone numbers, and monitor debts owed to suppliers (Deni).

Manager vs Cashier RBAC

Protect confidential financial records. Grant employees (Recorders) access only to POS screens while restricting Analytics and Settings to the Manager.

Tax & Profit Analytics

Track business year terms, calculate Cost of Goods (Opening + Purchases - Closing Stock), gross profit, operational expenses, damages, and taxable profit.

ESMA AI (Gemini Powered)

An integrated AI advisor that reads real JSON snapshots of your business database to provide health scores, answers in English or Kiswahili, and PDF reports.
